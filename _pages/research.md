## Psychometrics & Educational Measurement

- **Test Development & Validation**  
  My research involves designing and validating educational tests and surveys. I focus on methods to ensure reliability and validity across diverse populations.

- **Item Response Theory (IRT)**  
  I have extensive experience with IRT-based models, using them to enhance test scoring, item banking, and adaptive testing procedures.

- **Equating & Standard Setting**  
  I study various equating methods to compare scores from different test forms, as well as procedures to establish and maintain standards over time.

## Latent Variable Modeling

- **Structural Equation Modeling (SEM)**  
  My work explores relationships among latent constructs, allowing for more precise estimation of factors like motivation, engagement, and academic performance.

- **Multilevel Modeling**  
  I utilize hierarchical and multilevel approaches for complex data structures (e.g., student–classroom–school), accounting for nested data and contextual effects.

- **Bayesian Approaches**  
  When classical estimation techniques prove restrictive, I apply Bayesian methods for robust inference and more flexible model specification.

**Select Achievements**  
- Developed SEM frameworks to measure latent traits in K–12 education, referenced by policy-makers in [Region/State].  
- Awarded a grant from [Funding Agency] to investigate Bayesian methods for large-scale educational datasets.

## Sequential Analysis

- **Sequential Hypothesis Testing**  
  I employ sequential testing procedures to monitor data as it accumulates, enabling faster and more efficient decision-making in experimental and educational contexts.

- **Adaptive Designs**  
  My research includes creating adaptive sampling methods that reduce costs and improve power when investigating emerging or evolving phenomena.

- **Real-Time Intervention Tracking**  
  I focus on techniques for real-time monitoring of interventions, adjusting strategies on-the-fly to optimize outcomes in both educational and psychological studies.

**Select Achievements**  
- Implemented sequential designs in school-based intervention studies, reducing overall data-collection time while maintaining statistical rigor.  
- Collaborated with [Research Lab/Team] to develop an adaptive testing platform that responds to student performance in real time.
